Hi, I'm Shashank — a software engineer currently working at [Hevo Data](https://hevodata.com/). This is my personal notebook: a space where I document the things I learn, mostly around distributed systems, the JVM, and performance engineering.

## What I write about

Most posts here fall into two buckets:

- **Distributed systems** — working through consensus algorithms (Paxos, Multi-Paxos, Raft), storage systems (Bigtable, Snowflake), and the papers behind them.
- **JVM & performance** — profiling, JIT behaviour, and the kind of low-level debugging that comes up while working on high-throughput data pipelines.

I also keep a running list of [papers I've read](/papers/) with my own notes.
